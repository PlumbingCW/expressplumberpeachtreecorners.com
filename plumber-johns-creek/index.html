Johns Creek Emergency? Do This Now

These steps matter most in the first ten minutes.

  1. Close the main shutoff valve. It may be in the garage, a basement or an outdoor box. Turn the handle clockwise until it will not move any farther.
  2. Open the lowest tap in the house. Water drains and pressure falls, which slows the leak.
  3. Turn off power to any wet areas. Use the breaker panel and keep your feet dry while you do it.
  4. Switch off the water heater at its breaker or gas control. A tank that loses its supply can overheat.
  5. Call (770) 214-4545. Our line is open all night and on holidays.

If you smell gas, go outside before you call anyone. A burst pipe can wait a few minutes before you call. Gas cannot wait at all.

Houses That Followed Technology Park

Johns Creek grew around a business park, and the plumbing grew with the homes.

Technology Park and the community of Johns Creek began in 1981, and housing followed over the next decades. The city incorporated on December 1, 2006, and more than 82,000 people lived there at the 2020 census. Neighborhoods such as those around St. Ives Country Club and Newtown Park span a wide range of ages, so no single plumbing problem dominates.

Older homes lean toward aging water heaters and original fixtures. Newer ones lean toward pressure issues and hidden leaks in finished walls. A leak detection visit fits both, because it finds the water without opening every wall.
