Pipe Burst? Do These Five Things Immediately

The first ten minutes decide how much damage you get.

  1. Shut off the main water valve. Gwinnett County's advice during a freeze was to locate and shut off the main right away, then call a plumber. Know where yours is before winter.
  2. Switch off power to wet areas. If water reaches outlets or appliances, turn off the breaker from a dry spot.
  3. Open faucets at the lowest and highest points. This drains the lines and relieves pressure.
  4. Move belongings and photograph the damage. Insurers want proof of what you lost.
  5. Call (770) 214-4545. Dispatch answers nights, weekends and holidays.

If a pipe burst because of cold, other pipes may be frozen. Keep faucets cracked and heat on until a plumber checks the rest.

Why Pipes Burst in Georgia

Cold snaps are rare here, and that is part of the problem.

Freezing is the most famous cause, and December 2022 showed how it plays out. Johns Creek shut off water near Haynes Bridge Road on Christmas morning. Roswell Fire reported more than 100 burst-pipe calls in one day. Many homes in this area are not built with deep freeze protection because hard freezes are infrequent.

Freezing is not the only cause, since high water pressure, corroded fittings, shifting ground and aging pipe also lead to bursts. A pressure-reducing valve that has failed can stress every pipe in the house, and checking pressure at a front spigot is a quick way to see whether that is happening.

Winter Burst Pipes in Peachtree Corners

Local housing and utilities shape the response.

Water in Peachtree Corners comes through a Gwinnett County meter in the front yard. Pipes on the house side are the owner's responsibility, so a burst inside the house is a private repair. The county does not control water temperature or pressure inside your home, and the home's pressure-reducing valve is also yours to maintain.

In a cold snap, the pipes most at risk run through garages, crawlspaces, attics and exterior walls. Insulating them and letting faucets drip slowly on the coldest nights helps. A burst in a multifamily building can flood floors below, so managers should know the shutoff for each riser.

If the break is outside the house, our water line repair crews trace the line from the meter.

Before the next cold snap, walk the house and find the exposed pipes, then wrap them with foam insulation and seal gaps where cold air enters. Know where the main shutoff is and make sure every adult in the home can reach it quickly, because that single habit limits most burst-pipe damage to a manageable cleanup instead of a gutted room.

How We Repair a Burst Pipe

We stop the damage, then fix the cause.

  1. We isolate the break. The plumber closes valves upstream of the failure and confirms no water is flowing.
  2. We find the full extent. A burst often hides cracks nearby, so we check the line on both sides.
  3. We repair or replace the section. Copper, PEX or CPVC is cut out and replaced with matching material and proper fittings.
  4. We test under pressure. The line is brought up to pressure and watched for leaks.
  5. We advise on prevention. Insulation, pressure and shutoff upgrades are explained before we leave.

Replacement pipe and fittings should meet Georgia's state minimum plumbing code, which follows the 2024 International Plumbing Code (IPC). We pick materials and joints that code accepts for your type of line, so the repair holds up at inspection if one is needed.

Document the Damage for Your Insurer

Good records speed up a claim.

Take photos and video of the burst pipe, the water and every damaged item before cleanup. Keep the damaged pipe section if the plumber removes it. Save our invoice and notes, which describe the cause and the repair.

Call your insurance company soon. Policies differ on what they cover, so ask what documentation they want. We cannot speak for your insurer, but we can provide a written description of the work.

If water reached walls or flooring, mold can start within a couple of days. A drying contractor should check wet materials promptly.
