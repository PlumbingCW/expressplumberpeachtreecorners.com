What to Have Ready When You Call

A few details help dispatch send the right crew.

Have your street address and nearest cross street ready. Describe what is leaking or blocked, and say whether the water is still running right now. Tell the dispatcher about any smells, especially gas or sewage.

If you can, note where your main shutoff valve is. Plumbers can shut it themselves, but knowing the spot saves a few minutes.

Tell us whether anyone in the home is elderly, ill or an infant, because loss of water or hot water matters more in those households. Mention pets that might be loose when the plumber arrives, and say whether a gate or building door needs a code. Apartment and condo residents should also give the building name and unit number.

Visiting the Office

Plumbing work happens at your property, so the office is mostly for dispatch.

Our office is at 3495 Holcomb Bridge Rd in Peachtree Corners, and the map at the bottom of this page shows the spot. Crews leave from here for jobs across the area, so the office is not set up as a showroom. If you need to drop something off or talk in person, call first so a person is there to meet you.

We do not ask you to visit before a repair. The plumber comes to you, looks at the problem and explains the options at your home, where the facts are in front of both of you.
