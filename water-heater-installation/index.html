Water Heater Installation in Peachtree Corners, GA

A water heater is an appliance you notice only when it fails, so a careful install pays off for years. We set new tank and tankless units and finish every connection to a plan.

What a Proper Installation Includes

The visible tank is the smallest part of the job.

A good install is a set of connected details that all have to be right. The tank sits level on a base or in a drain pan, new flexible connectors replace the old ones and a temperature and pressure relief valve is piped to a safe discharge. The shutoff valve must also be accessible and in good working condition.

Gas units get a flue check and a leak test at every gas joint, and electric units get a dedicated circuit check with correct wire sizing. We also look at whether an expansion tank is needed, because a closed plumbing system builds pressure as water heats and has nowhere to send it.

Anything we find outside the original scope is explained to you before we touch it, which keeps surprises off your bill and out of your walls. If you have questions about a part or a step, ask while we are on site and we will walk you through it.

Choosing the Right Heater for the House

Match the type to your fuel, space and hot water habits.

Heater options at a glance

Heat pump.Basements or garages with open air.About 1,000 cubic feet of air and a 40 to 90 degree location.

The Department of Energy says heat pump water heaters are two to three times as efficient as standard electric resistance models. They need space and the right temperature range to deliver that. For on-demand heating, see our tankless water heater page.

Gas, Venting and Electrical Basics

These are the details that affect safety.

Gas appliances produce exhaust that must leave the building. A flue that is disconnected, sagging or blocked can send carbon monoxide into living space, so we inspect it on every gas install. We test each gas connection for leaks before we leave.

If you ever smell gas near a heater, leave the building at once and call Atlanta Gas Light at 877.427.4321 or 911 from outside. Do not turn on lights or use a phone inside.

Electric heaters need a circuit sized for their load. An undersized breaker or wire is a fire risk. We confirm the circuit and tell you when an electrician is needed.

Plan Your Install Day

A few steps make the visit smooth.

  1. Clear a path: Remove stored items around the heater and along the route to the door.
  2. Know your shutoff: Show us where the main water valve is, or ask us to find it.
  3. Plan for a short water outage: Water to the house is off for part of the visit.
  4. Tell us how you use hot water: Household size and long showers help us size the unit.
  5. Ask about the old heater: We haul it away as part of the job.

What Changes the Price of an Installation

Equipment and site conditions set the number.

  • Type and capacityTankless and heat pump units cost more than standard tanks.
  • Venting and gas workNew venting or a larger gas line adds labor and materials.
  • LocationA move to a new room changes piping, venting and electrical work.
  • Code itemsRelief valve piping, pans and expansion tanks add to the parts list.

Every price is explained before work begins. If something changes the scope, we stop and talk to you first.
