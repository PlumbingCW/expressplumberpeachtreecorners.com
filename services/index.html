How to Pick the Right Service

Start with the symptom, not the name of the repair.

Most people search for a symptom. The water is cold, the drain is slow or the ceiling is stained. Each of those points to a service page above, and each page explains what the plumber checks first.

Emergency tags mark the work that tends to come in at night or on weekends. Planned work covers jobs you can schedule, such as an installation or a pipe replacement. The line between them blurs quickly, because a slow drain on Friday can become a backup on Saturday night.

If you cannot tell which page fits, call. A dispatcher will ask a few questions about what you see, hear and smell, and then route the call to the right crew.

Emergency Work and Planned Work

Both start with the same phone number.

Emergency work means water, sewage or heat is already a problem. We aim to stop the damage first and fix the cause second. Planned work means the system still functions but needs attention before it fails.

Water heaters show the difference well. A leaking tank at midnight is an emergency, and our water heater replacement crew handles it. A tank that is old but working can be swapped on a schedule through water heater installation.

Pipes work the same way. A burst line needs burst pipe repair right now. A house full of aging supply lines may justify repiping before something fails.

What Every Visit Includes

The basics do not change from service to service.

You get a plain-language explanation of what the plumber found. You hear the price before work begins, and you decide whether to go ahead. The plumber tests the repair before leaving and cleans up the work area.

Related topics such as backflow, grease traps and cast iron pipe come up inside the service pages above rather than on pages of their own. Our commercial plumbing page covers the building-side topics, and the sewer line repair page covers older drain pipe.

If your problem is outside the list, call anyway. Dispatch will tell you honestly whether we can help, and we would rather say no than send the wrong crew.
