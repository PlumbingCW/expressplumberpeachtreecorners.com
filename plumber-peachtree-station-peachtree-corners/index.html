General Steps When Water Is Escaping

General guidance for any house, not specific to this community.

  1. Close the main shutoff. It sits where the water line enters the house. The county meter in the front yard is a separate point and belongs to the water utility.
  2. Turn off the water heater. Use the breaker, or set a gas unit to pilot.
  3. Stay out of wet rooms with live outlets. Cut power from a dry spot at the panel.
  4. Photograph the damage. Pictures help later if you file a claim.
  5. Call (770) 214-4545..

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 from outside.
