What You Will Find Here

Guides that answer the question before you call.

Each guide starts with the answer, then explains the reasoning. Topics range from running toilets and slow sinks to water heater swaps and slab leaks. We chose them because homeowners ask about them again and again.

You will not find price tables here, because prices depend on your house, your access and the hour. Instead, each guide names the factors that move the price so you can ask better questions when you call anyone.

The guides also separate what a homeowner can do from what needs a license. Replacing a showerhead or testing a toilet flapper is a safe weekend project. Cutting into a supply line or touching a gas appliance is not.

How We Write These Guides

Plain language and checked facts.

The Express Plumbing team writes every guide. Local facts come from public sources such as the Gwinnett County Department of Water Resources and the City of Peachtree Corners. When we are unsure about a rule, we say so and point you to the agency that decides.

Each guide links to the service pages that match its topic. Those links are there so you can see how the work is done, not to push you toward a call.

When a Guide Is Not Enough

Some problems cannot wait for reading time.

Active flooding, sewage backing up into the house and any smell of gas are emergencies. Shut off the water, leave the building if gas is involved and call for help. Our services page lists the work we handle, and the 24/7 line answers all night.

For a leak you can see but cannot find, our leak detection service traces the source without tearing out walls.

Questions About These Guides

No. They explain how a problem works and what you can safely try. Anything involving gas, active flooding, sewage or electrical risk needs a licensed professional, and the 24/7 line is open for exactly that.

Prices depend on access, parts, the scope of the repair and the hour of the call. A number printed in a guide would be wrong for most readers. Each guide names the factors so you can ask better questions.
