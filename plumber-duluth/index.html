Water Running Where It Should Not Be? Do This First

Stop the flow, protect the electrics and then call.

  1. Find the main valve and close it right away. It is usually near the water heater, in a basement or crawl space, or at the meter box outside.
  2. Open a faucet at the lowest point in the house. The lines drain and pressure drops, which slows the leak.
  3. Shut off the breaker for any wet areas. Do this from a dry position, and never touch switches while standing in water.
  4. Move rugs, electronics and paper goods to higher ground. Saving belongings matters more than mopping at this stage.
  5. Call (770) 214-4545. Our dispatch team works overnight and through every holiday of the year.

A sewage backup is a different kind of emergency. Keep everyone out of the affected room and wash well after any contact.

Before We Arrive: Duluth Preparation Cards

Small steps help the plumber start work the moment the truck pulls in.

Find the cleanout outside

Locate the capped sewer cleanout outside the house if you can. Clear leaves and mulch away from it so the plumber can open it.

Free up the work area

Empty the cabinet under the sink or the closet around the water heater, so there is room to kneel.

Photograph all of the damage

Take clear pictures of any wet drywall, ceilings or floors for your insurance records.

List every symptom you notice

Write down which drains are slow, when the smell began and whether the water is cold, rusty or both.
