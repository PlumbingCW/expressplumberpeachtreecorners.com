Dunwoody Is in DeKalb County, So Who Do You Call?

The county line changes the utility, and the utility changes the phone number.

Dunwoody incorporated on December 1, 2008, and it sits in DeKalb County. Water and sewer there come from DeKalb County Watershed Management, not from Gwinnett. That matters in practice. If water is gushing from the street or a main has failed, the county has to fix it. Emergency Steps While a Plumber Is on the Way

Do these five things in order and keep the whole routine simple.

  1. Shut the main valve as soon as you can reach it. Check the basement, the utility room or the outside meter box, and turn the handle fully clockwise.
  2. Drain the lines to relieve the pressure. Open the lowest faucet, and flush a toilet once if the supply is already off, to relieve pressure.
  3. Kill power to any flooded areas of the house. Flip the breaker for those rooms while standing somewhere completely dry.
  4. Protect valuables. Lift furniture legs onto foil or plastic bags and move electronics to a higher shelf or another room.
  5. Call (770) 214-4545. We take calls all night long and on every holiday.

For a gas smell, leave first and call from outside. Never turn on a light or start a car in the garage.
